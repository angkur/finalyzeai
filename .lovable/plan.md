# FinalyzeAI — 90-day traffic plan

The honest diagnosis: the site has good tools but almost nothing that ranks, and no distribution. SEO alone will not move numbers this month. So this plan splits into "things I build" and "things only you can do".

## Where you are

- ~51 visitors/30 days, 48 direct (you and people you messaged).
- Calculator + tool landing pages went live recently; Google has barely crawled them.
- Bounce 78%, 2.08 pages/visit — arrivals do not find a reason to stay.

## Part 1 — Make each visit worth more (build first)

1. **Instant free tool on the homepage.** Replace the hero's "Try AI Predict" dead-end with a working mini analyzer above the fold: paste revenue/expenses/cash, get a Founder Health Score with no signup. Signup is offered only after the result.
2. **Shareable result pages.** Every calculator and health-score run gets a public `/r/:id` URL with the numbers baked in, an OG image-worthy summary, and a "run yours" CTA. One user becomes several, and each share is an indexable page.
3. **Exit-intent / end-of-result CTA** on calculator pages: email the result as a PDF, which also captures a list you can market to later.

## Part 2 — Rank for things people actually search (build second)

4. **8–10 more calculators** on low-competition long-tail terms (SaaS magic number, CAC payback, LTV:CAC, rule of 40, dilution, ESOP value, invoice factoring cost, working capital). Same template as the existing six, so it is cheap to add.
5. **Comparison + "vs" pages**, e.g. burn rate vs runway, DCF vs comparables, gross margin vs contribution margin. These rank fast and feed the calculators.
6. **Internal linking pass**: every blog post links to its matching calculator, every calculator links to two related guides and one tool page.
7. **Sitemap + metadata audit** after each batch so nothing ships unindexed.

## Part 3 — Distribution (only you can do this; SEO is 3–6 months)

- Post one free calculator per week in r/SaaS, r/startups, r/smallbusiness, Indie Hackers — as a free tool, never as a product pitch.
- Answer 3 finance questions per day on Reddit/Quora/LinkedIn with a link only when it genuinely answers.
- Launch on Product Hunt and BetaList once the homepage tool is live.
- Submit the sitemap and request indexing for all new URLs in Search Console the day they publish.

## Part 4 — Measure

- Weekly check: indexed page count, impressions, clicks (Search Console), bounce and pages/visit (analytics).
- Kill anything with zero impressions after 60 days; double down on whatever gets any.

## Suggested order

1. Homepage instant tool (biggest bounce fix)
2. Shareable result pages
3. Calculator batch #2 (8–10 new)
4. Comparison pages + internal linking

## Out of scope

Paid ads, AdSense re-review timing, and anything requiring your Google/Reddit accounts — those are yours to action.
